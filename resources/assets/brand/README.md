# Google Classroom icon

`classroom-icon.png` is Google's official Classroom icon, unaltered: `32x32_yellow_stroke_icon@2x.png` (the classic green and yellow icon, 64 × 64 px, shown at 32 px) from Google's square logo pack:
https://developers.google.com/static/workspace/classroom/assets/classroom_square_logo.zip

It is used only on the Share to Google Classroom button. Google's Classroom branding guidelines (https://developers.google.com/workspace/classroom/brand) say not to change the icon or make your own, and to show it at 32 to 96 px with its aspect ratio kept. Replace it only with another file from Google's pack.
