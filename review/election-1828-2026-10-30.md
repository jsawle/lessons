# Fact-check and persona review: The Election of 1828 and the Right to Vote (30 October 2026)

Lesson draft 0.1.0 and map spec 0.3.0, checked by an independent fact-checker (17 problems: [lesson-factcheck.json](../maps/data/election-1828/research/lesson-factcheck.json)) and reviewed by five personas from the packets a teacher or student would see, with map pictures, the pop-up text for all 24 states and deuteranopia simulations: T3 Luis, T4 Ana, T5 Ruth, S1 Jordan, S3 Eli. The personas were told which issues every lesson shares (decisions A to J in [land-cessions-2026-10-30.md](land-cessions-2026-10-30.md)) and asked not to repeat them. Raw findings: [election-1828-2026-10-30/](election-1828-2026-10-30/). Nothing stopped the lesson. Every finding was checked against the lesson and the data before a change.
