# Persona review pilot, 22 October 2026

Seven personas (see [personas.md](personas.md)) reviewed two lessons, **1775–1776 Revolutionary Battles** and **We the People, 1787–1791**, each working only from what that person would see (built with `build_packets.py` from build 0.19.0). They raised 139 findings: 11 "stops the lesson", 86 "slows it down", 42 minor. 47 depend on the live map, which no reviewer could open. Every finding below was checked against the lesson files; the raw findings are in [pilot-2026-10-22/](pilot-2026-10-22/).

## Is the method worth it?

Yes, with changes.

- **It found real problems that earlier checks missed.** At least six are plain errors a teacher would hit (1 to 6 below), and several personas found the same ones independently.
- **Each persona earned its place except one pair.** The Texas teacher found the factual and standards problems, the teacher with many English learners and the Spanish-speaking student found the Spanish and map-language problems, and the teacher with shared devices found the paper and projector problems. The veteran and the first-year teacher overlapped heavily and could be one reviewer.
- **Its blind spot is the map.** A third of the findings can't be judged without the live map, and the known map defects (such as the wrong victor on Turning Points circle 1) were invisible to it. Next time the packets should include screenshots of the map, the legend and a few pop-ups.
- **The text packets caused two false findings** (question numbers, and a missing link, which is only missing on paper). The packet builder needs to show numbering and links as they appear.

## To check on the live map

- Revolutionary Battles question 3: can students name the unlabelled ports from the coastline and nearby diamonds? (S1, S2, T1, T3)
- Revolutionary Battles answer table: "8 battles in 1775, 13 in 1776" only adds up if Fort Ticonderoga and Crown Point, and Sullivan's Island and Breach Inlet, are two diamonds each. (T2)
- Diamonds overlapping at the zoom question 2 asks for, and how far students must zoom for question 4's circles. (T1, T2, T3)
- We the People: do steps that point to "the first line" or "the last two lines" of a pop-up still work on a narrow Chromebook screen? (T2, S2)
- We the People question 2: do the population pies hide the seat labels when both layers are on? (T2, S2)

## Not supported

- "The questions have no numbers" and "(numbered) prints after each heading" (S1): the text packet showed numbering that way; the Word sheet numbers the questions.
- "The sheet has no link to the map" (S1, S2): the Word sheet has a live link; it is only missing on paper (G).
